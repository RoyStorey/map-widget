This page shows a map the way a host site embeds it: inside the reading column, with its own timeline and chapters, themed by the page.

Paragraphs of the host's lesson come before and after the figure. Scroll past the map: the page scrolls normally, and Ctrl/⌘ + scroll (or two fingers) zooms the map.

The host decides where maps appear and what surrounds them; the map only tells its part of the story.
